# Copy

Words are design content. Generic copy makes a well-designed page read as a template, and it's
the tell people notice fastest.

## Rules

1. **Specific beats impressive.** "Beylikdüzü'ne ortalama 22 dakikada geliyoruz" beats
   "Hızlı ve güvenilir hizmet". "Find a flat in Berlin without agents" beats "Revolutionize your housing journey".
2. **The customer's words, the customer's questions.** Headlines answer what they'd ask on the phone.
3. **Numbers with units**, formatted for the locale: `₺1.250`, `45 dk`, `7/24`, `3,5 km`.
4. **Buttons say what happens:** "Hemen ara: 0532 …", "Masayı ayırt", "Save changes". The same
   action keeps the same name through the flow (button "Publish" → toast "Published").
5. **Cut adjectives that can't be checked:** quality, professional, reliable, premium, innovative.
   Replace with the fact that would prove it, or delete.
6. **One idea per heading.** No triads, no "Not just X, it's Y".
7. **Errors and empty states give direction:** what happened, how to fix it. No apologies, no
   "Oops!", no mascots saying "Nothing here yet!".
8. **Sentence case**, plain verbs, short sentences. Read aloud; rewrite what you wouldn't say to a person.
9. **Never invent proof:** testimonials, ratings, counts, logos, awards. Use `[FILL: real quote from customer]`.
